V — Process

How a problem gets solved

The same six steps, whether it's a dead circuit board, a broken server, or a misbehaving plugin.

01

Identify the symptoms

Understand exactly what's failing, and when.

02

Understand the system

Learn how the hardware or software should behave.

03

Form a hypothesis

Narrow down likely causes before touching anything.

04

Test & isolate

Check individual components or settings, one at a time.

05

Research & implement

Use documentation and community knowledge to apply a fix.

06

Verify & adjust

Confirm the fix holds, and refine it if it doesn't.
